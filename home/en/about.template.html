谢雨卿

I’m Xie Yuqing, also known as Carl. I was born in Mianyang, Sichuan, in 2000, spent my childhood in Haiyan, Zhejiang, went back to Sichuan for middle and high school, and moved to Beijing for university in 2020. I graduated from the University of Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and I’m now doing an MBA at City University Malaysia in Kuala Lumpur.

My name contains the character for rain, so my WeChat column is called 谢雨聊. I started writing in September 2024 about history, economics, cities and everyday life. Until October 2026 most essays appeared on WeChat first; since then they appear here first, and most have an English translation.

In my spare time I read, write and explore AI tools. I also walk 10,000 steps a day, a habit of two years that even earned its own essay, The Great Walk Theory.

This site holds my writing, the small tools I make, the places I have been and the photos I took on the way.
