雨聊

Rainy days are good for a chat.

I’m Xie Yuqing (Carl). I was born in Sichuan, spent my childhood in Zhejiang, went back to Sichuan for school, studied at university in Beijing, and I’m now doing an MBA in Kuala Lumpur.

My name contains the character for rain, 雨, so my WeChat column is called 谢雨聊, “Yuliao”, or rainy-day talks: history, economics and everyday city life. I have been writing since 2024; all {{chinese_total}} essays are here, most with an English translation.
