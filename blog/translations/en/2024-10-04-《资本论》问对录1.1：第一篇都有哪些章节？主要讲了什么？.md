# Questions and Answers on Capital 1.1: What Chapters Are in Part One, and What Do They Cover?

**Question:**

What chapters are in Part One of *Capital*, and what do they mainly cover?

**Answer:**

Part One is titled “Commodities and Money” and contains three chapters: “Commodities,” “The Process of Exchange,” and “Money, or the Circulation of Commodities.” This article discusses the first chapter, “Commodities.”
